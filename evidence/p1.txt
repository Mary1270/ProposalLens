DAO Demo Proposal 1: Community workshop funding

Type of change: treasury spend, a one-time payment from the DAO treasury.
Requested amount: 5,000 USDC to be paid from the treasury.
Recipients: 2 recipient addresses (the organizer wallet and the venue wallet).
Execution delay: the payment executes after a 48 hour timelock.
Privileges: this proposal does not grant any admin, minter or upgrade powers.
Audit: not applicable, there are no contract code changes.
